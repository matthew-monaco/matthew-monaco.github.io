<p><div style="text-align: justify"><span style="color: green">M<font size="2">ATTHEW</font> M<font size="2">ONACO</font></span> (b. 1997, New Jersey) is a Paris-based composer whose work spans notated composition, improvisation, and game pieces. Drawing on jazz and contemporary classical traditions, he explores the interplay between spontaneous musical decisions and deliberately constructed forms.</div></p> 

<p><div style="text-align: justify">Monaco studied composition at the New England Conservatory in Boston with Stratis Minakakis, earning his Bachelor's degree in 2020. He then moved to Paris to study with Stefano Gervasoni at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique et de Danse de Paris (CNSMDP), receiving his Master’s degree in 2022. There, he studied electronic music with Yan Maresz, Luis Naon, and Grégoire Lorieux. Seeking to deepen his engagement with electroacoustic music while revisiting his roots in improvised traditions, Monaco pursued a PhD in composition at the University of California, Berkeley. There, he worked with Carmine-Emanuele Cella and Edmund Campion at the Center for New Music and Audio Technologies (CNMAT), as well as with Myra Melford, with whom he further developed his improvisational practice in the tradition of American free jazz, a central influence on his musical language.</div></p>  

<p><div style="text-align: justify">Monaco’s compositions have been performed in the United States, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Slovenia, and Cyprus. His music has been presented by ensembles including Ensemble intercontemporain, Ensemble Itinéraire, Ensemble Court-circuit, Ensemble Linea, the Callithumpian Consort, the Del Sol Quartet, the Brouwer Trio, Patsiaoura Ensemble, and the Orchestre des lauréats du Conservatoire (CNSMDP). His works have been conducted by Hugh Wolff, Jean Deroyer, Jean-Philippe Wurtz, Stephen Drury, David Loebel, and Léo Margue, and performed by soloists including Alain Billard, Théo Ould, and Maria Eleonora Caminada. Monaco’s electroacoustic music has been premiered and performed at venues and institutions including Ircam, Stanford University’s CCRMA, and UC Berkeley’s CNMAT.</div></p>  

<p><div style="text-align: justify">Highlights of the 2026–2027 season include premieres in Graz, Luxembourg City, San Francisco, and Paris, as well as the Italian premiere of “Thread” by Divertimento Ensemble. Monaco is also participating in AVEC (Ateliers voix et composition) with the Cris de Paris and the “Unanimes !” orchestral mentorship program organized by the Association Française des Orchestres. Alongside these activities, he is completing his doctoral thesis on musical grammar in the solo improvisations of Cecil Taylor.</div></p>  

<p><div style="text-align: justify">Monaco lives in Paris as the recipient of the George Ladd Prix de Paris.</div></p>
